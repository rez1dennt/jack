# Image generation prompt set

All six raster assets were generated with the built-in image generator. The supplied landing screenshot was used only as a reference for subject category, composition, lighting, and the white/blue/red art direction. Every prompt required an original unbranded machine, no copied pixels, no readable labels, no logo, no text, and no watermark.

## `hero-machine`

Photorealistic ultra-wide industrial landing-page hero: a large white computer-controlled pattern sewing machine with restrained blue mechanical accents, flat metal table, and a dark touchscreen on the right. Bright blurred garment factory; high-key commercial product photography. Machine fills the right 62%; the left 38% remains airy and low-detail for the Russian headline.

## `solution-denim`

Photorealistic close 4:3 process view: a white and blue programmable sewing head stitching a precise pocket-shaped contour in warm orange-brown thread on dark indigo denim held in a rectangular clamp. Crisp needle, hardware, and fabric detail in a bright modern factory.

## `case-machine`

Photorealistic 4:3 case-study view: a programmable white and blue sewing machine stitching a reinforced rectangular pattern on charcoal technical fabric. Medium three-quarter framing, stainless work surface, premium catalog lighting, darker than the denim image.

## `spec-machine`

Photorealistic full catalog product view: a complete white automated pattern sewing workstation on an enclosed industrial cabinet base, broad brushed-metal table, right-side touchscreen, visible feet and controls, restrained blue accents, seamless pure-white studio background, three-quarter perspective, no cropped edges.

## `cta-operator`

Photorealistic production engineer seen mostly from behind at a factory workstation. A large monitor shows an unbranded CAD-like line drawing of a sewing machine and stitch path. Dark navy and charcoal scene with cool screen light; person at the left edge, monitor centered-right; tight 4:3 crop for a CTA band.

## `footer-stitch`

Photorealistic dark 2:1 macro: polished automated sewing needle and presser hardware above black technical fabric, with a precise red-orange rectangular stitch. Mechanics occupy the upper-right third; the left half remains nearly black and low-detail for the footer gradient.

## Post-processing

ImageMagick was used to auto-orient, resize, strip metadata, and export AVIF, WebP, and fallback JPEG/PNG files. The Open Graph crop is 1200×630.
